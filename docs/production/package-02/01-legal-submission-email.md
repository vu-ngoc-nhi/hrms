**Subject:** Legal review needed before EmeSoft's HR system holds real employee data (Mini HRS) — answers requested by [date]

EmeSoft has built an internal HR records system, Mini HRS, for about 70–100 current and former employees. It is ready technically, but it has only ever held synthetic test data. **We will not enter any real employee data until we have your written advice** on the attached questions. Several of them (lawful basis, retention, hosting, impact assessment) are preconditions that no technical control can replace.

The attached pack describes the system, the data it holds and does not hold (no ID numbers, bank, salary, health or family data; no export; no AI processing), and the questions.

**Decisions we need from you**
